How to Safely Recover a Frightened Toy Poodle

Toy poodles entering survival mode exhibit high flight drive and sensory sensitivity. Follow these rules carefully.

1. Never Run or Chase

Running towards a frightened toy poodle reinforces panic and pushes them across dangerous arterial traffic roads. Stop in place immediately.

2. Gentle Crouching Posture

Turn sideways, drop to one knee, and avoid direct predatory eye contact. Drop fragrant treats on the pavement and speak in calm, gentle tones.

3. Familiar Sound Cue

Use high-pitched, positive whistle tones or squeaky toy cues. High toy poodle intelligence triggers auditory recall even under elevated adrenaline.
