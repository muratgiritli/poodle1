Quality Control & Canine Safety

The yourpoodle Salon Certification Standard

Every groomer on yourpoodle is vetted in-person on breed-specific anatomy, stress metrics, and precision scissoring dexterity. Never compromise on your toy poodle's safety.

100% Cage-Free Protocol

Zero cages, drying cabinets, or unmonitored isolation. Salons must operate dedicated 1-on-1 private suites with owner presence options or live camera feeds.

Trachea & Coat Anatomy

Toy poodles possess sensitive tracheas and high-density wool coats. All stylists undergo strict training against tight nooses, blade burn, and heat damage.

Digital Lookbook Sync

Eliminate miscommunication. Lookbook spec sheets automatically send ear length, muzzle geometry, and guard height measurements directly to the stylist's iPad.
