Teacup puppy lethargy after 4 hours without food: Distinguishing hypoglycemia from normal deep sleep

🐩Koko (@koko_teacup)Teacup White Poodle • 9 weeks old • 680 grams

Koko hasn’t eaten her noon kibble mush. At 4:00 PM she was unresponsive to calling her name, limp when lifted, but started weakly licking when nudged. How do we quickly assess blood sugar collapse vs normal puppy REM sleep, and what is the exact emergency glucose dose?

Dr. Selin Aktaş, DVM

Head of Neonatal & Pediatric Canine Unit • Board Certified Genetics

Immediate 3-Step Emergency Action for Puppies Under 1.0kg

Gingival Glucose Smear: Rub 1 ml of Nutri-Cal paste or 100% pure maple syrup directly onto upper gums with a clean finger. Do not force liquids down the throat if limp.

Thermal Stabilization: Poodle puppies cannot metabolize glucose if hypothermic. Wrap immediately in a warm towel (heated with a hairdryer, not hot) against your bare skin.

Target: Alertness should improve within 7-10 minutes. Follow up immediately with warm puppy milk replacer (Esbilac). If unresponsive after 10 min, transport to the nearest ICU immediately.

How to manage Grade 2 Patellar Luxation in an 11-month-old Toy Poodle without immediate surgery?

🐩Mochi (@aylin.poodle)Apricot Toy Poodle • 11 mos • 2.85 kg • Bilateral Medial LuxationX-Rays & Orthopedic Report Attached

Mochi occasionally “skips” on her left hind leg after running or jumping off the living room sofa. Her orthopedic exam showed Grade 2 bilateral medial patella luxation with shallow femoral trochlear grooves. Our local clinic mentioned surgery is possible, but we want to know what conservative regimens (UC-II, hydrotherapy, weight limits) can safely delay or avoid sulcoplasty.

Dr. Mert Sarper, DVM, PhD

Resident, European College of Veterinary Surgeons (ECVS) • Small Canine Orthopedics Specialist

Top 1% Clinical Contributor

In an 11-month-old toy poodle weighing 2.85 kg, Grade 2 medial patellar luxation (MPL) does not automatically mandate immediate surgical intervention unless there is progressive cartilage wear, tibial crest rotation, or cranial cruciate ligament (CCL) instability.

1. Chondroprotective Protocol

Begin undenatured type II collagen (UC-II, 10mg daily) paired with cold-extracted New Zealand green-lipped mussel (Perna canaliculus) for high ETA omega-3 anti-inflammatory support.

2. Weight & Biomechanical Guard

Keep Mochi strictly under 2.90 kg. Every 100g of excess weight multiplies femoropatellar stress by 4x during trotting. Use rubber stair runners and eliminate bed jumping.

When Sulcoplasty is Non-Negotiable: If the skipping episodes exceed 4-5 times per day, if unilateral lameness persists for over 48 hours, or if follow-up radiographs at 18 months reveal femoral trochlear flattening, trochleoplasty with lateral retinacular imbrication is curative with a 98% success rate in poodles under 3kg.

Stubborn reddish-brown tear stains on Apricot Poodle coat: Porphyrin vs. clogged nasolacrimal ducts

🐩Apricot household7-month-old apricot toy poodle, purified reverse-osmosis water, ceramic bowls.

We have switched to ceramic bowls and purified reverse-osmosis water, but our 7-month-old apricot toy poodle still wakes up with moist rusty fur beneath both medial canthi. Could this be juvenile entropion, micro-tearing from teething, or porphyrin oxidation?

Dr. Canan Erdem, MRCVS

Veterinary Dermatologist & Ophthalmic Fellow

Between 6 and 9 months of age, teething causes mild inflammation around the maxillary nerve roots, compressing the tear drainage canal. Additionally, Malassezia yeast feeds on porphyrins in the damp fur. Have your vet perform a simple Fluorescein Dye Test to confirm duct patency before buying over-the-counter bleaches. Clean twice daily with hypochlorous acid (HOCl) veterinary ophthalmic spray.
