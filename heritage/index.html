Genomic Chromatics

Coat Color Genetics & The "Clearing" (Açılma) Phase

Poodle hair pigment is dictated by the precise epistatic interaction between Eumelanin (black/brown) and Phaeomelanin (red/yellow). As hair follicles mature, progressive silvering genes and oxidation produce the renowned French clearing spectrum.

The 24-Month "Clearing" (Açılma) Trajectory

Chronological pigment attenuation from early neonatal density to adult phenotypic permanence.

Non-Fading Lines Maintain 85%+ Rufus Saturation

1

Neonatal (0 – 8 Wks)

Silvers appear identical to solid black; apricots appear rich burnt cinnamon.

2

Pastern Shift (4 – 6 Mos)

Groomers shave base pads. A platinum or peach halo emerges at the root while guard tips remain dark.

3

Coat Change (9 – 14 Mos)

Soft puppy fluff sheds into dense adult helical curls. Silvering accelerates through shoulders and muzzle.

4

Adult Baseline (24 – 36 Mos)

True platinum silver, warm champagne cream, or a permanent café-au-lait tone remains stable.

Follicular Dermatology

Hair vs. Fur: The Molecular Physics of the Single-Coat

Unlike 95% of Canis familiaris breeds equipped with compound double-coats that shed cycling underwool into the environment, Poodles possess continuous-growth human-like keratin filaments.

Double-Coat Fur

Short anagen growth cycle ending in a seasonal blown undercoat.

Lightweight dander flakes become airborne carriers for salivary allergens.

High shed volume requiring continuous vacuum mitigation.

Poodle Single-Coat Hair

Prolonged anagen phase; hair strands grow continuously up to 20 cm.

Dead follicles curl inward into the spring matrix rather than shedding freely.

Minimal salivary Can f 1 dispersion across household airflow.
